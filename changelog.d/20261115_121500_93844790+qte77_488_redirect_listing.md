### Fixed

- `refresh` and the source re-probe now treat a redirect from a posting URL to its board listing (the posting's parent path, or an `?error=true` bounce, as Greenhouse sends for a closed posting) as gone (reported as 404). Same-posting redirects (http to https, trailing slash, canonical id) still count as live. The probe sees the redirect only on the httpx and curl tiers, which do not follow redirects; a fetch escalated to the patchright tier still follows it silently.
