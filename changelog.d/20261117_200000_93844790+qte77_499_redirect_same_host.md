### Fixed

- The board-listing redirect check (#488) now needs the redirect to stay on the posting's own host with an http(s) scheme, for the parent-path rule and the `?error=true` rule alike. A posting or feed that redirects to another host, even to its root or an ancestor path, keeps its 3xx status instead of being reported as gone (404). Tests cover cross-host, same-host root, empty `Location` and feed-probe redirects (#499).
