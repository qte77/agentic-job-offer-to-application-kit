### Fixed

- `persist` ties each screened row to the batch record it was screened from (`results/batches/batch-*.json`) and takes the id, url, company and title from that record, not from the screen agent. A row whose id matches no record falls back to an exact company + title match; rows that still match nothing are listed in the summary and not stored. A result that matches no record, or a missing `results/batches/`, fails before anything is written (#513).
- `persist --merge` removes a stored row that a re-screened batch read but did not keep, as a fresh screen would, and prints the count. Only the batches the result lists in `screenedBatches` count (the relevance workflow and `collect-relevance` now write it); a result without it, or with an unmatched row, removes nothing and says why (#515).
