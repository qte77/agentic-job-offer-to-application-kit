### Added

- `ajoa-kit drift-check` re-renders every offer pack from its `result.json` and reports each one as `clean`, `drifted` (naming the files), `no source` (packs persisted before `result.json` was kept; not a failure) or `invalid source`. It compares the five rendered artifacts and `coverage-report.md`; the review sidecars and `meta.json` also depend on other inputs and are skipped. Report only: nothing in a pack is written or deleted. Exits 1 when a pack needs attention.
