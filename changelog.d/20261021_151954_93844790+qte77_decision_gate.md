### Added

- `chunk --decision-gate` asks the TypeSafe Jev decision model whether each JD fits any lane and leaves confident misfits (probability below 0.25) out of the relevance batches. In the 2026-10-21 shadow test that threshold kept all 118 score-4+ offers and skipped about 30% of JDs. A failed call keeps the JD, and every skip is listed with its score in `results/decision-prefilter/skipped.jsonl`.
