### Added

- Each offer pack's `meta.json` now records `evidence_library`, a short sha256 of the `evidence-library.json` it was tailored against, so a pack tailored against an older library build is visible after a rebuild.
- `ajoa-kit evidence-diff OLD [NEW]` prints both library stamps and the top-level fields a rebuild added, removed or changed (`NEW` defaults to `results/evidence-library.json`).
