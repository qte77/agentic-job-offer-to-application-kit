### Added

- `persist-offer` keeps each pack's source as `results/offers/<slug>/result.json` and stamps the rendered `.md` frontmatter with `generated_from: "result.json"`, so a hand-edited artifact can be re-rendered with `persist-offer <dir>/result.json --slug <slug>` (#365).

### Changed

- Offer `meta.json` is written and read through a pydantic `OfferMeta` model (parse on read, ADR-0003).
